Delete your Dual Fight account

Dual Fight, made by Jayanta Datta.

Option 1: inside the game (instant)

  1. Open Dual Fight and tap ACCOUNT on the main menu.
  2. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm when asked. You are asked to confirm three times, because it cannot be undone.

This works for registered and guest accounts. Please do this before you uninstall the game: a guest account has no email address, so we cannot find it afterwards.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the game, send an email from the email address of your account to jayantadatta2005@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Dual Fight account. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password), your profile, your coins and experience points, your items and upgrade levels, and your match rewards: deleted.
  • Match records that involve only you: deleted. A record you shared with another player stays in their history with your account ID removed.
  • Connection logs kept by our hosting providers are not in our control and follow their own retention periods.
