Privacy Policy

Dual Fight (the "game"), made by Jayanta Datta ("we", "us"). Effective 13 November 2026.

This policy explains what the game collects, why, where it is kept and how to delete it. We keep it short on purpose. The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases for real money.

1. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Email address and password, only if you create an account. The password is stored by our sign-in provider in scrambled (hashed) form; we never see it.So you can sign in and keep your progress.
A random account ID. This is all a guest account has: no email, no name.To save the progress of that account.
Game progress: coins, experience points, items you own and their upgrade levels.To give you the same items every time you play, and to stop cheating.
Match records: a match ID, the two account IDs, who won, how long it lasted and when.To pay match rewards exactly once and to keep a match history.
The in-game name you choose.Your opponent sees it during a match. It is kept only in the running match on our game server, not in our database.
Your IP address and connection details.Our hosting providers see them whenever your phone connects, which is needed to deliver the game over the internet. We do not use them to identify or track you.

On your phone the game also saves your sign-in session, your sound setting and the server address in the app's private storage. That data never leaves your phone unless it is part of the above.

The app asks Android for one permission only: access to the internet. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising ID.

2. Who handles the data

  • Supabase (supabase.com) provides sign-in and our database. Our project is hosted in Southeast Asia (Singapore).
  • Render (render.com) runs the game server that connects players. It may keep ordinary connection logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy.
  • GitHub Pages (github.com) hosts these web pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under its own policy.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else. We may disclose it if the law requires us to.

3. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, items, rewards and sign-in are removed at once. Match records that involve only you are removed; a record you shared with another player stays for their history with your account ID removed from it. Connection logs kept by our hosting providers follow their own retention periods.

4. Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

5. Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers (HTTPS and secure WebSockets) in the released app. Players can only read their own account rows, and only our game server can change coins and items. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app open ACCOUNT, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm, or see how to delete your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to receive a copy. Write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change matters a lot, we will also tell you inside the game.
